Frequently Asked Questions

What is the process for buying land through Nezona Properties?

Our process is simple and transparent. It begins with a site inspection, followed by verification of documents, payment, and finally, the allocation of your plot and delivery of all necessary title documents.

Are your properties certified and free from government acquisition?

Absolutely. We conduct rigorous due diligence on all our properties. Every parcel of land we sell comes with a Certificate of Occupancy (C of O) or other valid government-approved title, ensuring it is secure and free from any encumbrances.

What payment plans are available for your estates?

We offer flexible payment structures to suit different financial capabilities, including outright payment and installment plans spanning from 3 to 12 months. Please contact our sales team for a plan tailored to your needs.

Can I start developing my land immediately after purchase?

Yes. Once your payment is complete and you have been physically allocated your plot, you can commence development, subject to obtaining the necessary building plan approvals from the relevant authorities.
